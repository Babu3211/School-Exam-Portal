Development-only school-scoped attendance. School Admin may save attendance; a Teacher with an active class attendance assignment may create one student's attendance at a time (no overwrite); Viewer is read-only. Class attendance is saved in chunks of 10; a failure can leave earlier chunks saved. No BS date conversion or IEMIS sync yet.
